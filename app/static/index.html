Answer quality

Lookups wrong and Ungrounded are different failures, and the distinction is the point. The baseline's wrong answers were not blanks: asked for First American's total revenue it found a table with five revenue columns, summed three, showed its working, and reported $6,810.3m against a true $7,452.2m. That figure appears nowhere in the passages, so Step 13 flags it — three such answers at baseline, none after table-aware chunking. The final configuration's three wrong lookups are a different error: every figure is present in the evidence, read from the right table on the wrong row. No grounding check can catch that, which is why both columns are shown.
